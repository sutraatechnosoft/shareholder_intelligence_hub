KPIX Dashboard

Executive Financial KPIs & Real-Time Dashboards

KPIX Dashboard consolidates accounting engine data (account.move.line, account.move, account.account) into a high-performance pre-calculated reporting model. Avoid heavy, real-time live aggregations on your ledger every time a dashboard or financial report loads.

Key Business Benefits

Optimized Performance

Pre-calculated KPI snapshots mean instantaneous screen loads, removing database lag during board meetings or executive reviews.

Accurate Financial Metrics

Get precise insights into EBITDA (strictly isolating Depreciation & Amortization from OPEX), ROE, ROA, aging reports, and cash flow.

Automated Background Cron

Configure hourly or daily snapshots per company automatically via scheduled actions with robust indexing on company records.

Strict Granular Security

Includes dedicated read-only Executive/Shareholder groups with robust multi-company record-level rules (ir.rule).

Designed for Community & Enterprise

Depends solely on the core 'account' module, ensuring full compatibility without requiring external dependencies.
